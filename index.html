Jasper the Cat

The fluffiest friend who ruled hearts and couches alike

About Jasper

Jasper was no ordinary cat — he was a king in fur. Born in 2015, he quickly became known for his regal demeanor, endless curiosity, and his uncanny ability to find the coziest corners in the house. Whether he was chasing laser dots, demanding chin scratches, or staring contemplatively out the window, Jasper made every moment special.

His favorite foods were tuna treats and anything stolen off the counter. Jasper loved curling up in laundry baskets, watching birds from the windowsill, and meowing loudly during Zoom calls to make sure he got attention.

Though Jasper has crossed the rainbow bridge, his purrs still echo in the hearts of everyone who loved him.
